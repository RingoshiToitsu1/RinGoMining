My story

I learned mining the hard way

Most people pitching Bitcoin mining have never touched a miner. I owned a whole facility. Here's what that taught me.

  1. The meme coin that changed everything

    In 2021 I put money into Shiba Inu and turned it into over a million dollars.

  2. I went all in on real machines

    I took that money, bought physical Bitcoin miners, and built my own mining facility in Rochester, Michigan.

  3. The power bill started winning

    When Bitcoin's price dropped hard, my electricity costs ate the profits. I shipped the machines to a partner facility in Toluca, Mexico for cheaper power.

  4. Then it caught fire

    The Toluca facility went up in flames. My machines were destroyed. Worse, a technician working there was badly burned and lost his life. That stays with me.

  5. I found a better way in

    I wanted to stay in Bitcoin mining without owning machines that can break, burn, or hurt someone. I researched cloud mining, didn't like what I found, and landed on GoMining. I've been mining with them ever since.

The real cost. This is what physical mining can do when something goes wrong. A man was hurt doing his job at that facility, and he didn't make it home.

I share this out of respect, not for shock. It's the reason I'll never run machines again, and the reason I tell people exactly what they're getting into.
