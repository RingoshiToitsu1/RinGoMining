Why the split matters

Every GoMining miner pays a daily electricity and maintenance fee. Locking GoMining tokens lowers that fee. On my account the discount is 28.22%. Locked tokens also earn staking rewards, with an APR of 21.7% right now (updated Oct 7, 2026).

So if you put everything into terahash, you pay full fees and miss the staking income. If you put everything into tokens, you're not mining. The right answer is a mix, and the right mix depends on your budget. That's the math the Optimizer does for you.

  • Start with today's Bitcoin price. That's your baseline.
