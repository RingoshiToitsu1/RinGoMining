GoMining fees explained: the honest version

Anybody pitching GoMining without talking about fees isn't being straight with you. On my account, the daily fee takes about half of my gross rewards. Here's how it works and how I keep it down.

By Ringo · 10+ years in crypto · mining on GoMining since May 2025

I'm going to be real with you, because nobody else seems to be. GoMining fees are not small. On my account, the daily fee takes about half of my gross rewards. I'm still profitable, and I keep adding terahash, but you should know what you're walking into.

The daily electricity and maintenance fee

When you buy terahash on GoMining, you own a share of real mining machines running in real facilities. Those machines use power and need maintenance. So every day, your miner pays an electricity and maintenance fee out of the Bitcoin it mined.

What you see in the app is gross rewards, then the fee, then what you keep. Always look at what you keep.

On the day I checked in October 2026: about $265 gross, about $128 kept, on roughly 6,834 TH.

What makes your fee bigger or smaller

  • Efficiency (W/TH). How many watts your miner uses per terahash. Lower is better, and it lowers your electricity cost. I run at 15.41 W/TH.
  • Your maintenance discount. Locking GoMining tokens lowers your fee. Mine is 28.22%.
  • VIP level. Higher VIP levels get a maintenance discount too.
  • Bitcoin's price. The fee is a cost; rewards move with Bitcoin. When Bitcoin is low, the fee eats a bigger share. When it climbs, you keep more.

The other costs

  • Buying terahash. About $18.99 per TH when I last checked. Prices move with the market.
  • Withdrawing. You pay a network fee when you send crypto out. Here's how I withdraw.
  • Reselling. You can sell a miner, but they only resell at a discount. Treat terahash as a long-term thing.

Only invest money you can afford to lose. That's how I think about it with my own money.

FAQ

What fees does GoMining charge?

Every miner pays a daily electricity and maintenance fee, taken out of its Bitcoin rewards. That's the fee that matters. You also pay to buy terahash in the first place, and network fees when you withdraw.

How much of my rewards do GoMining fees take?

On my account, on the day I checked in October 2026, the fee took roughly half of my gross rewards: about $265 gross, about $128 kept. It depends on your efficiency, your discount, and Bitcoin's price.

How do I lower my GoMining fees?

Lock GoMining tokens for the maintenance discount (mine is 28.22%), upgrade your miner's efficiency (lower watts per terahash), and use VIP discounts. Code RINGO5 includes a month of VIP Platinum+.

Can I sell my GoMining miner?

Yes, but miners only resell at a discount, so don't count on getting back what you paid. Only invest money you can afford to lose.
