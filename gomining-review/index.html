Gross vs net: what you actually keep

This is the part most reviews skip. The big number at the top of my dashboard, $265.69 for one day, is gross. GoMining charges a daily electricity and maintenance fee. After that fee, my daily reward was about $128.

You can see it in the reward rows under the chart: $128.32, $131.95, $137.91. That's what lands in my account each day. (I take my rewards in GoMining tokens, which is why those rows show GOMINING amounts.)

Gross is what GoMining mines for you. Net is what you keep. Always look at net.
