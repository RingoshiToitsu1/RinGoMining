What it does that other calculators don't

Most GoMining calculators take a terahash number and spit out a daily reward. That's not how you should actually invest. The Optimizer starts from your budget instead:

  1. You enter a dollar amount. $100, $1,000, $100,000, whatever you're working with.
  2. You set the Bitcoin price. Use today's price, or test a scenario. What if Bitcoin hits $150,000? Type it in and watch the projection change.
  3. It splits your money the smart way. Part goes into terahash to mine Bitcoin. Part goes into GoMining tokens, locked, to hit the maximum maintenance and electricity discount and earn the weekly staking rewards.
  4. It projects your ROI over 12 months, and two, three, and four years, based on the current Bitcoin price and current rewards.

How to read your results

  • Start with today's Bitcoin price. That's your baseline.
  • Then test a higher price. Mining is a long game; the upside is in what your stacked Bitcoin is worth later.
  • Then test a lower one. Know what the slow stretch looks like before it happens. When rewards got thin on my account, the discount and staking rewards are what kept me profitable.
  • Look at year two to four, not just month one. Reinvested rewards compound.

Projections are based on current prices and rewards, which change daily. They're not promises. Not financial advice.
