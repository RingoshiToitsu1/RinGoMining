When I tell people I mine Bitcoin on GoMining, the first thing they ask is: "OK, but can you actually get the money out?" Fair question. I had the same one. So here's exactly what I do, the same way I'd show you if you were sitting next to me.

Option 1: To my bank account

This is my route when I want cash in the bank:

  1. Withdraw on BNB Smart Chain. In GoMining, I withdraw my rewards using the BNB Smart Chain network. GoMining shows you the network fee before you confirm.
  2. Swap to USDC. USDC is a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar, so the value doesn't bounce around while it's moving.
  3. Send the USDC to Coinbase. Coinbase gives you a deposit address. Copy it exactly.
  4. Withdraw to my bank. From Coinbase, I sell or cash out the USDC and send it to my bank account.

That's it. Once you've done it once, it takes a few minutes.

Don't make these mistakes

  • Wrong network. This is the big one. Before you send anything, check that the network the receiving side shows matches the network you're sending on. Crypto sent on the wrong network can be lost for good.
  • Skipping the test. The first time you use a new address, send a small amount first. Once it lands, send the rest.
  • Typing addresses by hand. Always copy and paste, then check the first and last few characters.
  • Forgetting taxes. Mining rewards and cashing out can be taxable where you live. Keep records.
