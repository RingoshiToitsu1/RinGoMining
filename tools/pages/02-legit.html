GoMining is not cloud mining

Most people who call GoMining a scam are lumping it in with cloud mining and calling it a day. If they did the research, they'd see it's a different thing:

  • Cloud mining sells you a contract, usually 12 months. You earn for that period, and the money you put in is locked. When the contract ends, it's over.
  • GoMining sells you a digital miner with no end date. You earn from it for as long as you own it. You can upgrade its power and efficiency. And you can sell it on the marketplace to get some of your money back out.

That exit is the big one. Scams don't build a way for you to leave.

The honest risk

I'm not going to pretend there's zero risk. The one thing that can really go wrong with GoMining is GoMining ceasing to exist. For that to happen, Bitcoin would have to go down really, really far, for a long time.

I know what that looks like from the inside. When I mined on my own machines, we started with Bitcoin around $70,000 and watched it drop below $20,000. It became so unprofitable that we had to stop and ship our miners to a partner facility in Toluca, Mexico, for cheaper power.

Then that facility caught fire. All my machines were destroyed, and a technician working there was badly burned and lost his life. I'll never forget it.

That's how I found GoMining: I wanted to stay in Bitcoin mining without owning machines that can break, burn, or hurt someone.

So yes, the risk is a catastrophic Bitcoin collapse bad enough to take GoMining down with it, or GoMining going out of business for some other reason. We can only look at their track record. We can't see the future. I trust the track record and where they're going, and that's why I'm an investor today.
