Holding gives you one thing

When you buy Bitcoin and hold it, you get exactly one thing: Bitcoin's price. If it goes up, you win. If it goes sideways, your money sits there. Nothing else happens.

When you put the same money into GoMining the way I do, you get four things working at once:

  1. Bitcoin, every single day. Your terahash mines Bitcoin rewards daily, so you're stacking more of it whether the price is up or down.
  2. GoMining token upside. Part of your money goes into GoMining tokens, the token of the company actually running the mining.
  3. Yield. Locked tokens earn staking rewards, {{APR}} APR right now, and cut your mining fees.
  4. Liquidity. Your terahash isn't stuck. You can sell it on the marketplace to get money back out.

How I split my money: 60 / 40

Here's the strategy. Roughly 60% of my capital goes into terahash, the part that mines Bitcoin every day. Roughly 40% goes into GoMining tokens, locked, which gets me the maximum maintenance discount (mine is 28.22%) and the weekly staking rewards.

Only the 60% is in terahash, and even that can be sold on the marketplace for liquidity. That's a very different setup from a mining contract that locks your money for a year.

Getting in on the ground floor

The GoMining token is how you own a piece of the company's growth, not just Bitcoin's. GoMining has been around since 2017, has run its digital miners since 2021, and operates real facilities you can watch on video.

In my opinion, the GoMining token is undervalued right now. That's a big part of why 40% of my money sits in it.

Holding only Bitcoin gives you zero exposure to that.
